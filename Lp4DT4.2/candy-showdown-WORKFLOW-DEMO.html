Valentine's Day Candy

Valentine's Day candy leans romantic and a little fancy — boxed chocolates, heart shapes, and candy meant to be given as a gift rather than grabbed by the handful.

  • Heart-shaped boxes of assorted chocolates
  • Conversation hearts with printed messages
  • Chocolate-covered strawberries
  • Cinnamon hearts

Halloween Candy

Halloween candy is built for volume — fun-size bars, individually wrapped pieces, and anything that survives a pillowcase full of trick-or-treating.

  • Fun-size chocolate bars
  • Candy corn
  • Lollipops
  • Gummy worms and spiders
