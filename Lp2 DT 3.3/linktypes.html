Wisconsin Fall Colors Getaway Weekend

Join Maple & Miles Travel Co. for a relaxing weekend exploring the beautiful fall colors of Wisconsin.

Our three-day getaway includes scenic drives, hiking, local food, and plenty of opportunities to enjoy the Wisconsin outdoors.

Trip Details

Dates

October 16–18, 2026

Destination

Northern Wisconsin

Price

Starting at $499 per person.

What's Included

  • Two nights of lodging
  • Guided fall color hike
  • Scenic driving tour
  • Local food tasting
  • Free time for shopping and exploring

Weekend Activities

Scenic Drive

Travel through Wisconsin's forests and small towns while enjoying some of the best fall colors of the season.

Guided Hike

Take a guided hike through a Wisconsin state park and enjoy views of forests, lakes, and wildlife.

Local Flavors

Visit local restaurants, coffee shops, and markets to experience some Wisconsin favorites.

Free Time

Spend part of the weekend exploring local shops, taking photographs, or simply relaxing.
